I make things with care, whether that's an interface or a cake.


  

    
My path into design started with curiosity about how websites work, why some apps feel effortless and others don't, and what makes a piece of writing or imagery genuinely move someone. That curiosity led me to user experience design, where I get to combine research, problem-solving, and visual craft.


    
I'm drawn to projects that have a little weight to them. Work that helps people, or quietly improves something they didn't even realise was broken. My recent project, Walk of Life AI, was a chance to do exactly that: designing an AI-powered tool to help students build CVs and portfolios they actually feel proud of.


    
Outside of school and studio work, I bake. A lot. What started as a lockdown hobby slowly grew into something more: a small home-baking business called Yam's Oven. It's taught me as much about design as any class has: patience, iteration, attention to small details, and the importance of making something beautiful and useful at the same time.

  







  

    

      
Yam's Oven


      
"Bespoke cakes and cupcakes, made with care."


      
Yam's Oven is my home-based cake studio specialising in bespoke cakes and cupcakes, where aesthetics and decor are just as important as the taste.


      
What started as weekend experiments grew into something I now share with friends, neighbours, and beyond. Every order is designed and decorated by hand, because a cake should be as beautiful as it is delicious.
